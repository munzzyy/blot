Privacy

Blot has no server side, so this is the whole policy:

  • Your documents are processed on your device and never uploaded. The page opens no connections except to load its own files, and the Android app has no internet permission at all.
  • Nothing you open is stored. Documents live in memory while you work and are gone when you close them; the finished file goes exactly where you send it.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no cookies. The browser keeps one preference: your language.
